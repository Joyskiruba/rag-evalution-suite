# Billing FAQ

## Billing Cycle
All subscriptions are billed monthly in advance on the same calendar day the customer originally signed up. Annual plans are billed once per year and receive a 15% discount versus monthly pricing.

## Failed Payments
If a payment fails, the system retries automatically after 3 days, then again after 7 days. If both retries fail, the account is downgraded to the free tier and the customer is notified by email each time.

## Refund Policy
Refunds are only issued within 14 days of the original charge, and only for annual plans. Monthly plan charges are non-refundable but customers can cancel at any time to stop future charges.

## Plan Downgrades
Downgrading a plan takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Customers keep access to their current plan's features until that cycle ends, with no partial-period refund.

## Tax Handling
Sales tax is calculated automatically based on the billing address on file and is recalculated any time the billing address is updated.
