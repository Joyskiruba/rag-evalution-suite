# Data Retention Policy

## Customer Data Retention
Active customer account data is retained indefinitely while the subscription is active. After account cancellation, customer data is retained for 30 days to allow reactivation, then permanently deleted.

## Log Retention
Application logs are retained for 90 days for debugging purposes. Security audit logs are retained for 1 year to support compliance investigations.

## Backup Retention
Full database backups are taken nightly and retained for 35 days on a rolling basis. Monthly backups are additionally archived for 12 months for disaster recovery purposes.

## Data Deletion Requests
Customers who submit a formal data deletion request under privacy regulations receive full deletion within 30 days, except for financial records, which must be retained for 7 years for tax compliance regardless of the deletion request.

## Employee Data After Departure
Former employee records, including performance reviews, are retained for 7 years after departure in accordance with employment law requirements.
