# Travel and Expense Policy

## Expense Submission Deadline
All business expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within 21 calendar days of the purchase date. Expenses submitted after 21 days require director-level approval to be reimbursed.

## Meal Limits
Meal reimbursement while traveling on business is capped at $75 per day for domestic travel and $110 per day for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstance.

## Flight Booking Rules
Flights under 6 hours must be booked in economy class. Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy with manager pre-approval. Business class requires VP-level approval regardless of flight duration.

## Client Entertainment
Client entertainment expenses over $200 require pre-approval from a director before the event takes place, not after.

## Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expenses are reimbursed within 2 pay cycles of submission, typically appearing on the employee's paycheck within 14 to 28 days.
