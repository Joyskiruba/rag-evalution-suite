# Employee Onboarding Policy

## New Hire Setup Timeline
All new hires at Northwind SaaS must complete IT equipment setup within their first 2 business days. Laptops are provisioned by the IT team and shipped to arrive no later than the employee's start date. Access to Slack, email, and the internal wiki is granted automatically on day 1.

## Buddy Program
Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy from a different team for their first 30 days. The buddy is responsible for informal check-ins twice a week and is not the same person as the new hire's manager.

## Probation Period
All full-time hires undergo a 90-day probation period. A formal 30-day check-in and a 60-day check-in are conducted by the hiring manager. Confirmation of permanent employment happens at the 90-day mark following a written review.

## Required Training
New hires must complete the Security Awareness training module within their first 5 business days. Failure to complete this training locks access to production systems automatically.

## Equipment Return
Employees who leave the company must return all issued equipment within 10 business days of their last working day, or the cost is deducted from their final paycheck.
