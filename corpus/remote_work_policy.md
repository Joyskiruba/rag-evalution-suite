# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees are eligible for full remote work after completing their 90-day probation period. Before that, new hires are expected to work from a company office at least 3 days per week.

## Core Hours
All remote employees must be online and reachable between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their local timezone, regardless of which timezone their team is based in. This is called the "core overlap window."

## Home Office Stipend
Fully remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 during their first 60 days, and an annual refresh stipend of $150 every year after that.

## International Remote Work
Employees may work remotely from another country for a maximum of 45 consecutive days per calendar year without triggering a formal international employment review. Any longer stay requires approval from both HR and Legal.

## Equipment for Remote Staff
Remote employees are responsible for providing their own internet connection. The company does not reimburse home internet costs, only the one-time and annual stipends listed above.
