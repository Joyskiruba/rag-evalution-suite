# Password and Access Security Policy

## Password Requirements
All internal system passwords must be at least 14 characters long and cannot reuse any of the employee's last 10 passwords. Passwords expire and must be rotated every 180 days.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory for all employees accessing production systems. Employees have a 7-day grace period after account creation to enroll in MFA before access is automatically suspended.

## Privileged Access Reviews
Access to production databases is reviewed quarterly by the security team. Any access not used in the prior 90 days is automatically revoked and must be re-requested if needed.

## Lost Device Reporting
Employees must report a lost or stolen device within 4 hours of noticing it missing. IT will remotely wipe the device within 1 hour of the report being filed.

## Third-Party Access
External contractors may be granted system access for a maximum of 180 days per engagement before requiring a formal renewal and re-approval from the security team.
